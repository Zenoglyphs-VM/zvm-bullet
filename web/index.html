Russian Roulette in Telegram.

One bullet, one revolver, passed round the table. Whoever fires the bullet loses their stake to the survivors. Stakes are in BULLET, held by an escrow contract for the length of the game: the original Ethereum contracts, redeployed on the ZVM.

Open @{{bot}} Buy BULLET

Play

  1. DM @{{bot}} /connect. It replies with a secret number.
  2. Call connectAndApprove(secret) on the BULLET contract's Write tab, then send the bot the transaction hash.
  3. Buy BULLET with ZNN; the faucet hands out devnet ZNN.
  4. In the group, /roulette 6 50 starts a six-chamber game at 50 BULLET; /join takes a seat.
  5. /pull to fire, /pass to hand on. /leaderboard ranks the group.
  • A game waits two minutes or until every chamber has a player, and needs two.
  • /join 75 bets above the minimum, capped at what the rest of the table staked.
  • You must pull once before you may pass. Idle for 30 seconds and the bot pulls for you.
  • If every player votes /stop, every stake is refunded.

Provably fair

The chamber is drawn with a VRF when the game starts, committed in newGame, and everything needed to check it is revealed in endGame. 

alpha   = "<unix seconds>.<microseconds>,[bet1, bet2, ...]"
beta    = VRF(alpha)
chamber = beta mod revolver size        (the first pull fires chamber 0)
commit  = SHA-256( decimal(chamber) || salt )
zvm-bullet verify -record deployments/zvm.json <endGame transaction hash>

ECVRF-EDWARDS25519-SHA512-TAI (RFC 9381). The commitment fixes the chamber once play starts; it cannot stop the bot, which picks the moment in alpha, from trying moments first. That is why the bot's own wallets may never play.
